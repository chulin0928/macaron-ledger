Macaron Ledger — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Macaron Ledger ("the app") is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

The short version

Macaron Ledger does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

When you add an expense you may type an amount, choose a category, write an optional note, and pick a date. You may also choose a display currency and toggle haptic feedback in Settings. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, and no network requests of its own. We cannot see your expenses, and we cannot see that you use the app.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. On Android it may use the standard vibration capability for the optional haptic feedback when you save an expense; you can turn that off in Settings.

Backups

If your device backs itself up to iCloud or Google's backup service, the app's local data may be included in that backup under your own Apple or Google account, according to the settings you control on your device. We have no access to those backups.

Deleting your data

Open Settings inside the app and tap Erase all data, or simply uninstall the app. Either action removes all expense data from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Macaron Ledger is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Third-party links

The Settings screen links to this privacy policy and to a support email address. Opening a link uses your device's browser or mail app, whose own privacy policies apply.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated and the effective date changed. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
