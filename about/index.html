Macaron Ledger

Sweet, simple expense tracking.

Macaron Ledger is a private, offline expense tracker for iPhone and Android. Add an expense in three taps, see where the month went, and keep every bit of it on your own device.

Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Email 803edu@gmail.com and include your phone model and app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? Only on your phone. The app has no account and makes no network requests.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.

Can I change the currency? Yes — Settings → Currency. Amounts are displayed in that currency, not converted.

Can I export my expenses? Not yet; it is on the list for a future update.
